You are a **technical AI partner**, not a cheap freelancer.

## AI Business vs SaaS

- **Start with capital**
  - AI Business: Start with **$0**
  - SaaS: Need **min $5,000+** investment

- **Team**
  - AI Business: **Solo-friendly**
  - SaaS: Usually **only if dev** (or you must hire devs)

- **Skill requirements**
  - AI Business: **No tech background required** (you can orchestrate tools)
  - SaaS: **Tech background or hire dev**

- **Speed to money**
  - AI Business: **Fast income**
  - SaaS: **Delayed income** (build, then monetize)

- **Product–market fit risk**
  - AI Business: **PMF = 0 risk** (you sell what is already demanded)
  - SaaS: **PMF = high risk** (you must discover what works)

- **Customer type**
  - AI Business: Work with **Enterprise / B2B**
  - SaaS: Often work with **B2C**

- **Churn**
  - AI Business: **No churn rate** (project / retainer based)
  - SaaS: **Churn rate matters** (subscriptions)

## Core Economics & Offer Principles

- **Sell ROI, not “AI magic”**
  - Frame as **“dollars at a discount”**, not fancy tech.

- **Target businesses with budgets**
  - Ignore broke founders and idea people.
  - Go after operating companies with revenue and real problems.

- **Automate high-value areas**
  - Ops  
  - Marketing  
  - Tech  
  - Sales  

- **Pitch math**
  - Example: Employee at **$6k/mo** doing repetitive steps 1–5  
  - Your **$12k** automation → **$100k+/yr saved** in time and errors.

- **Pricing power**
  - They can comfortably spend **$5k–$20k** on your service / subscription if ROI is clear.

## Stage 1 — Strategy & Niche (Copy First)

- **Copy competitors; don’t invent from scratch.**
- Gather **5–10 competitors** in a **$$$ niche**.
- **Outplay by channels**:
  - If they run **YouTube ads**, you attack via **SEO / LinkedIn / Twitter**, etc.
- **Steal ads 1:1**:
  - Clone what works (rename / logo swap) to get **instant conversions**.
- **Run A/B tests**:
  - Test **cloned winner vs your variant**, then iterate visuals and offer.

---

## Stage 2 — Offer & Target (“Discounted Dollars”)

### Who You Target

- Operating businesses with:
  - Clear processes
  - Existing revenue
  - Staff already doing repetitive work
- Ignore:
  - Early-stage, broke, or “idea stage” founders.

### How You Position the Offer

- Translate every workflow into:
  - **Hours saved**
  - **Cost saved**
  - **Revenue added / protected**
- Make the offer feel like:
  - “Pay **$1** to reliably get **$3–$10** back.”
- Focus your promise on:
  - A specific department (Ops / Marketing / Tech / Sales)
  - A specific workflow (e.g., lead qualification, proposal drafting, email triage).

---

## Stage 3 — Positioning (Partner, Not Freelancer)

- You are a **Technical AI Partner**, not a **“$50/hr freelancer.”**
- Show **business-operations understanding** so you can charge a **premium** (e.g., **$200/hr** or high-ticket projects).
- Showcase your **design processes** (how you think and build systems) and **own outcomes** (before/after numbers).

---

## Stage 4 — First Proof / Portfolio (Door-Opener)

- Find **1 client** via:
  - Friends
  - Colleagues
  - Cold outreach
- Do work **free or minimal fee** strictly **in exchange for a testimonial**:
  - Tight scope
  - Clear deadline
- Capture:
  - **Video review** + **text review**
  - Include **numbers** (hours saved, revenue impact, etc.).
- Case example format:
  - “Saved **$135k**, paid **$5.7k**, automated sales flow using AI tools.”  
  - Include **company name** and **contact** (with permission).

## Stage 5 — Tools & Build Speed (to Ship Demos Fast)

- **Fast landing page / MVP**
  - Use **Lovable.dev** or **Bolt.new**  
  - Use **Cursor AI** if you’re more experienced.

- **Workflows**
  - Use **n8n** or **Make** for AI automations.  
  - Tons of YouTube tutorials to copy.

- **AI Palette**
  - **OpenAI**
  - **Gemini**
  - **Grok**
  - **DeepSeek**

- **Search & Agents**
  - Use **Search / DeepSearch / AI agents** where needed.  
  - Let agents **cross-check** each other.

- **Data compliance focus**
  - Double-check sensitive data flows.
  - No leaks.
  - Systems should be robust and **hard to trick**.

## Stage 6 — Lead Generation

### A) Upwork (not Fiverr)

- Use **scripts** (Python + Cloud/Claude/Cursor code) to **auto-reply** on relevant jobs.
- In every reply, **give free value upfront**:
  - Even a **mini build** or loom walkthrough.
- **Invest early**:
  - A small LP (landing page) build can lead to a **large contract** later.

### B) Cold DM (Twitter / LinkedIn / Email)

- Find **operating companies** (not wantrepreneurs).
- **DM consistently**; volume matters.
- Expect **1–5% replies per 1,000 messages**.
- Offer a **free audit**:
  - Sometimes **build one workflow for free** to demonstrate a **“one-click wow”** moment.

### C) Paid Ads (Copy What Works)

- Run **cloned competitor creatives**:
  - Same structure, **brand swapped**.
- Keep **testing**:
  - While your own ad creatives **iterate in parallel**.

---

## Stage 7 — Lead Conversion

- Always show your **portfolio** and at least **one real case study**.
- **Deep dive** into their operations:
  - Ask about their processes, tools, bottlenecks.
- **Do not hard-sell**:
  - Ask questions.
  - Propose tailored AI workflows and solutions.
- Send **offer after the meeting** and go through it **via call**.
- Use:
  - **Contract**
  - **Prepayment** (upfront or milestone-based).

---

## Stage 8 — Delivery & Scale

- **Automate real processes**:
  - Emails
  - Sorting and routing
  - CRM updates
  - Replies
  - Internal notifications
  - etc.
- Implement using **n8n / Make + LLMs**.
- Your role:
  - You **orchestrate** the systems.
  - **Hire freelancers** for micro-tasks (data cleanup, integrations, UI polish) when needed.

---

## Summary

- Start by **copying what already works** in a profitable niche.  
- Sell **clear, quantified ROI** to businesses who can pay.  
- Prove value with **one killer case study**, then scale with better tools, better positioning, and repeatable lead-gen.
