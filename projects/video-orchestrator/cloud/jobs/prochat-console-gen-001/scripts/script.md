# Why AI Won't Replace Founders

## Prompt
Why AI won't replace founders

## Status
This is an approved script ready for generation. Awaiting video assembly.

## Content
AI tools are transforming how founders build, but they won't replace the founder's core role: making decisions with incomplete information, taking calculated risks, and building culture. AI excels at acceleration and pattern recognition—perfect for execution. But vision, judgment, and conviction remain uniquely human.
