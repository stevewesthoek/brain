📄 ROUTINE BUNDLES FRAMEWORK

Version: v0.1
Date: 2026-02-27
Purpose: Convert library depth into structured value → pricing justification → SaaS bridge
Confidence: High (0.93)

⸻

Core Principle

People do not buy more stories.
They buy fewer decisions.

A bundle is:

A routine solved.

Not:
10 audios.

But:
A predictable bedtime flow.

⸻

Bundle Definition

A routine bundle is:

A curated sequence of audios across routine states that solves one emotional bedtime problem.

Structure:

START
TRANSITION
FALL_ASLEEP
NIGHT_WAKE

Every bundle must cover at least 2 of these states.

That ensures real utility.

⸻

Bundle Archetypes (Foundation)

Bundles emerge from taxonomy automatically.

1️⃣ Safety Bundle

Problem:
Child fears bedtime / separation

Components:
RESCUE narratives
Shepherd Psalms
Protection loops

States covered:
TRANSITION → FALL_ASLEEP → NIGHT_WAKE

This is your strongest early bundle.

⸻

2️⃣ Calm Bundle

Problem:
Overstimulated child

Components:
VERY_SOFT Psalms
Ambient scripture
Loop variants

States:
FALL_ASLEEP dominant

This becomes subscription anchor later.

⸻

3️⃣ Story Night Bundle

Problem:
Child needs narrative to settle

Components:
Genesis stories
Jonah
Joseph

States:
START → TRANSITION

This sells well early.

⸻

4️⃣ Night Wake Bundle

Problem:
Child wakes repeatedly

Components:
Short Psalms
Safety loops
Whisper variants

States:
NIGHT_WAKE only

High retention bundle.

⸻

5️⃣ Foundations Bundle

Problem:
Parent wants full starter routine

Components:
Creation
Noah
Shepherd
Rest

States:
All four

This is your flagship early bundle.

⸻

Bundle Construction Rules

Do not build ad hoc.
Even manual bundles must follow the taxonomy rubric.

Bundles must be:

Taxonomy-driven
State-balanced
Emotion-coherent
Energy-progressive

Each bundle should feel intentional, not random.

⸻

Minimum Bundle Criteria

A valid bundle must include:

At least 3 audios
At least 2 routine states
Clear emotional theme
Progressive energy drop

Without this, it is just a collection.

⸻

Pricing Logic

Bundles justify price increases without friction.

Early phase:

Single = $9
Bundle = $29–39

Mid phase:

Single rises
Bundle value increases automatically

Late phase:

Bundles become entry to subscription.

Key rule:
Bundle must always feel cheaper than decision fatigue.

⸻

Bundle Lifecycle

Bundles evolve.

Phase 1:
Manual curated bundles (taxonomy rubric required)

Phase 2:
Dynamic bundles (taxonomy driven)

Phase 3:
Personalized bundles (intelligence driven)

Same assets.
More value.

⸻

SaaS Bridge

Bundles are proto-playlists.

Playlists become routines.
Routines become adaptive routines.
Adaptive routines become subscription.

Bundles are the psychological bridge.

They teach:

“this is how bedtime flows.”

⸻

UX Pattern

Bundles should be presented as:

Problems solved.

Not:
Products.

Example language:

For anxious nights
For overstimulated evenings
For routine starters
For night wake support

Outcome framing converts.

⸻

Library Intelligence Interaction

The system should detect:

User repeatedly plays safety content
→ Suggest Safety Bundle

User uses many story items
→ Suggest Story Night Bundle

Bundles become recommendations, not promotions.

That reduces resistance.

⸻

Content Planning Impact

Bundles guide what to produce next.

If Safety bundle thin → create safety content
If Calm bundle strong → extend loops
If Night wake weak → produce short pieces

Bundles become roadmap feedback.

⸻

Technical Skeleton

Eventually each bundle is:

bundle_id
bundle_theme
included_audio_ids
routine_states
emotional_tags
recommended_sequence

That allows:

Dynamic pricing
Personalized bundles
Subscription gating

Design now avoids refactor later.

⸻

Failure Modes

Do not:

Create too many bundles early
Bundle by book only
Bundle randomly
Use marketing names without behavioral meaning
Overlap bundles excessively

Clarity > variety.
