# [040] Feeding the Five Thousand for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 040-matthew-feeding-five-thousand-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Five Thousand Fed
**Bible passage:** Matthew 14:13-21
**Angle:** Five loaves and two fish — what little we bring is enough in the hands of Jesus, a story of abundance from scarcity
**Story beat summary:** Jesus withdraws to a quiet place but crowds follow. He has compassion and heals the sick. Evening comes. The disciples want to send the people away. A boy has five loaves and two fish. Jesus blesses, breaks, and gives. Everyone eats and is satisfied. Twelve baskets of fragments remain. What was not enough became more than enough.
