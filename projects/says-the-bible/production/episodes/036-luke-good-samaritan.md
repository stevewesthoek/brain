# [036] The Good Samaritan Story for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 036-luke-good-samaritan-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Good Samaritan
**Bible passage:** Luke 10:25-37
**Angle:** The one who stopped — a story of unexpected mercy from the one least expected to show kindness
**Story beat summary:** A lawyer asks Jesus, "Who is my neighbor?" Jesus tells a story. A man falls among robbers on the road to Jericho. A priest passes by. A Levite passes by. A Samaritan stops, binds his wounds, carries him to an inn, and pays for his care. Jesus asks, "Which of these was neighbor to the man?" The answer: the one who showed mercy.
