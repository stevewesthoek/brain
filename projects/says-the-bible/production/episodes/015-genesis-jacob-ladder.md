# [015] Jacob's Ladder Story for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 015-genesis-jacob-ladder-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Jacob's Ladder
**Bible passage:** Genesis 28:10-22
**Angle:** A man alone on a stone pillow receives the promise of God's presence — a perfect sleep story of rest found in unexpected places
**Story beat summary:** Jacob flees from Esau, lies down with a stone for a pillow. He dreams of a ladder reaching to heaven with angels ascending and descending. God speaks the promise of land, descendants, and presence. Jacob wakes, names the place Bethel, and knows he was not alone.
