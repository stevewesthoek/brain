# [034] The Fiery Furnace Story for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 034-daniel-fiery-furnace-30m
**Thumbnail title:** The Furnace
**Bible passage:** Daniel 3
**Angle:** Three men who would not bow, thrown into fire and found walking with a fourth — God's presence in the flames
**Story beat summary:** Nebuchadnezzar builds a golden image and commands all to bow. Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego refuse. The king rages and heats the furnace seven times hotter. They are bound and thrown in. The king sees four figures walking unbound in the fire. He calls them out. Not a hair singed, no smell of smoke. God delivered His servants.
