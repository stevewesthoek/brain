# [016] Jacob and Esau Reconciled for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 016-genesis-jacob-esau-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Jacob & Esau
**Bible passage:** Genesis 32-33
**Angle:** The long night of wrestling and the embrace of forgiveness — reconciliation after years of fear
**Story beat summary:** Jacob returns toward his brother Esau after twenty years, afraid. He wrestles through the night with a mysterious figure and receives a new name. At dawn he limps forward. Esau runs to meet him and embraces him. The brothers weep and are reconciled.
