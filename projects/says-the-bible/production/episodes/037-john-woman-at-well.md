# [037] The Woman at the Well Story for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 037-john-woman-at-well-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Woman at the Well
**Bible passage:** John 4:1-42
**Angle:** A conversation at noon that changed everything — living water offered to one who came to draw from a well
**Story beat summary:** Jesus sits at Jacob's well at noon, tired from the journey. A Samaritan woman comes to draw water. He asks her for a drink. She is surprised. He speaks of living water that becomes a spring welling up to eternal life. He knows her story. She leaves her jar and tells the town. Many believe. The water He gives satisfies the deepest thirst.
