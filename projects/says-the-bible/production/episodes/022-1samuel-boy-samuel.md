# [022] The Boy Samuel Called by God for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 022-1samuel-boy-samuel-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Boy Samuel
**Bible passage:** 1 Samuel 3
**Angle:** A child lying in the dark hears his name called — God speaks in the quiet of the night, and a boy learns to listen
**Story beat summary:** Young Samuel serves in the tabernacle under Eli. At night, lying in the dark, he hears his name. He runs to Eli, who sends him back. It happens again. The third time, Eli understands and tells Samuel to say, "Speak, Lord, for your servant hears." Samuel listens, and God speaks.
