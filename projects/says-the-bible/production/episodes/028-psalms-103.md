# [028] Psalm 103 — Bless the Lord for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 028-psalms-103-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Bless the Lord
**Bible passage:** Psalm 103
**Angle:** A psalm of remembered mercy — from the personal to the cosmic, all of God's compassion gathered in one song
**Story beat summary:** David calls his soul to bless the Lord and forget not His benefits. God forgives, heals, redeems, crowns with steadfast love. He is merciful and gracious, slow to anger. As a father pities his children, so the Lord. As far as east is from west, so far He removes our sins. Our days are like grass, but God's love is from everlasting.
