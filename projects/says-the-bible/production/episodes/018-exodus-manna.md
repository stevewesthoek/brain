# [018] Manna from Heaven Story for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 018-exodus-manna-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Manna from Heaven
**Bible passage:** Exodus 16
**Angle:** God provides bread from heaven each morning — enough for the day, a rhythm of trust and daily provision
**Story beat summary:** Israel wanders in the wilderness and grows hungry. They grumble. God promises bread from heaven. Each morning, manna appears like frost on the ground — enough for the day. They gather it. On the sixth day, a double portion. On the seventh, rest. God provides day by day.
