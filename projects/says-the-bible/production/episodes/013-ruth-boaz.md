# 013 Ruth and Boaz Story for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 013-ruth-boaz-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Ruth & Boaz
**Bible passage:** Ruth 1–4
**Angle:** Focus on faithfulness, loyalty, and God's quiet provision — Ruth's devotion to Naomi and Boaz's kindness as redeemer
**Story beat summary:** It begins with loss in Moab — Naomi bereaved, Ruth choosing to stay. The journey to Bethlehem brings them to the barley harvest, where Ruth gleans in the fields of Boaz. Boaz notices her, protects her, and honours her faithfulness. At the threshing floor Ruth asks for his covering, and Boaz redeems her at the gate. The story closes with marriage, the birth of Obed, and the quiet assurance that God provides through ordinary faithfulness.
