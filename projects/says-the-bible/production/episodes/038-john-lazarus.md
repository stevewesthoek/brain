# [038] Lazarus Raised from the Dead for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 038-john-lazarus-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Lazarus Raised
**Bible passage:** John 11:1-44
**Angle:** Jesus weeps and then calls a dead man by name — the power of compassion and the authority over death, told gently for rest
**Story beat summary:** Lazarus is sick. His sisters Mary and Martha send word to Jesus. Jesus waits two days, then goes to Bethany. Lazarus has been dead four days. Martha says, "If you had been here, my brother would not have died." Jesus weeps. He goes to the tomb and calls, "Lazarus, come out." The dead man comes out, bound in linen. Jesus says, "Unbind him, and let him go."
