# [030] Psalm 139 — You Are Known for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 030-psalms-139-30m
**Thumbnail title:** You Are Known
**Bible passage:** Psalm 139
**Angle:** A psalm of being fully known and held — God's intimate knowledge of every moment, every thought, every hidden place
**Story beat summary:** The psalmist declares that God has searched and known him. God knows when he sits and rises, discerns his thoughts from afar. Where can he go from God's Spirit? If he ascends to heaven, God is there. If he makes his bed in the depths, God is there. He was knit together in his mother's womb. God's thoughts toward him are vast as the sand.
