# [039] The Lost Sheep Story for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 039-luke-lost-sheep-30m
**Thumbnail title:** The Lost Sheep
**Bible passage:** Luke 15:1-7
**Angle:** A shepherd who leaves ninety-nine to find the one — the relentless, personal, seeking love of God
**Story beat summary:** Tax collectors and sinners gather to hear Jesus. The Pharisees grumble. Jesus tells a parable: a shepherd has a hundred sheep and loses one. He leaves the ninety-nine in the open country and searches until he finds it. He lays it on his shoulders, rejoicing. He calls his friends to celebrate. There is more joy in heaven over one sinner who repents.
