# [025] Elijah Fed by Ravens Story for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 025-1kings-elijah-ravens-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Elijah & Ravens
**Bible passage:** 1 Kings 17:1-16
**Angle:** God provides in the wilderness through ravens and through a widow's jar that never runs empty — daily faithfulness in a time of drought
**Story beat summary:** Elijah declares a drought to King Ahab and flees to the brook Cherith. Ravens bring him bread and meat morning and evening. When the brook dries up, God sends him to a widow in Zarephath. She has only a handful of flour and a little oil, but shares it. The jar does not run empty, and the jug does not run dry, all through the drought.
