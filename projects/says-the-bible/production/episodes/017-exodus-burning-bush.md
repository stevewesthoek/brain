# [017] The Burning Bush Story for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 017-exodus-burning-bush-30m
**Thumbnail title:** The Burning Bush
**Bible passage:** Exodus 3-4
**Angle:** God speaks from fire that does not consume — a call given gently to a reluctant shepherd on holy ground
**Story beat summary:** Moses tends sheep in the wilderness near Horeb. He sees a bush burning but not consumed and turns aside. God speaks his name and reveals holy ground. The call to free Israel is given. Moses hesitates, God reassures. He goes forward carrying the staff and the promise.
