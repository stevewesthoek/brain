# [031] Fear Not — Isaiah's Promise for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 031-isaiah-fear-not-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Fear Not
**Bible passage:** Isaiah 41:10, 43:1-7, 44:1-5
**Angle:** The repeated "fear not" declarations of Isaiah — God calls by name, redeems, walks through fire and water beside His people
**Story beat summary:** God speaks through Isaiah to a people in exile: Fear not, for I am with you. I have called you by name, you are mine. When you pass through the waters, I will be with you. Through the rivers, they shall not overwhelm you. Through fire, you shall not be burned. You are precious in my eyes, and I love you. Do not fear.
