# [014] Isaac and Rebekah Story for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 014-genesis-isaac-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Isaac & Rebekah
**Bible passage:** Genesis 24
**Angle:** The quiet faithfulness of Abraham's servant finding Rebekah at the well — a story of prayer answered, trust rewarded, and love found in God's timing
**Story beat summary:** Abraham sends his servant to find a wife for Isaac. The servant prays at the well, Rebekah appears with kindness and generosity. She agrees to go, travels the long road, and meets Isaac in the field at evening. They are joined in love and comfort.
