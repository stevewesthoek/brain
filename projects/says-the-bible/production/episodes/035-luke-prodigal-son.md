# [035] The Prodigal Son Story for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 035-luke-prodigal-son-30m
**Thumbnail title:** The Prodigal Son
**Bible passage:** Luke 15:11-32
**Angle:** A father who watches the road and runs — extravagant welcome for one who was lost, a picture of God's patient love
**Story beat summary:** A younger son asks for his inheritance and leaves for a far country. He squanders everything and ends up feeding pigs. He comes to his senses and decides to return as a servant. But while still far off, the father sees him, runs, embraces him. The best robe, the ring, the feast. The son who was dead is alive; the one who was lost is found.
