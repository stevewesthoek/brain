# [023] David and Goliath Story for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 023-1samuel-david-goliath-30m
**Thumbnail title:** David & Goliath
**Bible passage:** 1 Samuel 17
**Angle:** A shepherd boy steps forward when warriors hold back — quiet courage rooted in the memory of God's faithfulness with the lion and the bear
**Story beat summary:** The armies of Israel face the Philistines. Goliath taunts them daily. David, the youngest, arrives with bread for his brothers. He volunteers. Saul offers armor; David takes his sling and five stones. He runs forward, strikes Goliath, and the giant falls. The battle is won by faith, not by sword.
