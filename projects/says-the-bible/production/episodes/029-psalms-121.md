# [029] Psalm 121 — The Lord Who Keeps You for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 029-psalms-121-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Psalm 121
**Bible passage:** Psalm 121
**Angle:** A pilgrim's song of protection — lifting eyes to the hills, knowing help comes from the Maker of heaven and earth
**Story beat summary:** The singer lifts eyes to the hills and asks where help comes from. Help comes from the Lord, maker of heaven and earth. He will not let your foot be moved. He who keeps you will not slumber. The Lord is your shade. The sun shall not strike you by day, nor the moon by night. The Lord will keep your going out and your coming in, now and forevermore.
