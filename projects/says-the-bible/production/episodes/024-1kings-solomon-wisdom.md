# [024] Solomon's Wisdom Story for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 024-1kings-solomon-wisdom-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Solomon's Wisdom
**Bible passage:** 1 Kings 3
**Angle:** A young king asked by God what he wants, and choosing wisdom over power or riches — a night-time dream that shaped a kingdom
**Story beat summary:** Solomon becomes king after David. At Gibeon, God appears in a dream and says, "Ask what I shall give you." Solomon asks for an understanding heart. God is pleased and grants wisdom, and riches and honor besides. Solomon awakes. The famous judgment of the two mothers follows, and all Israel sees that the wisdom of God is in him.
