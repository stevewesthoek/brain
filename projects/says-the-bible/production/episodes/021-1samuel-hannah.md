# [021] Hannah's Prayer Story for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 021-1samuel-hannah-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Hannah's Prayer
**Bible passage:** 1 Samuel 1-2
**Angle:** A woman pours out her heart silently before God and is heard — prayer in the deepest need, answered in the fullness of time
**Story beat summary:** Hannah is childless and grieved. She goes to the tabernacle and prays silently with moving lips. Eli mistakes her for drunk, but she explains her sorrow. God remembers her prayer. Samuel is born. She dedicates him to the Lord and sings a song of praise.
