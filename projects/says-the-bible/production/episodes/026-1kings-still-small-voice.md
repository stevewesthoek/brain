# [026] The Still Small Voice Story for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 026-1kings-still-small-voice-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Still Small Voice
**Bible passage:** 1 Kings 19
**Angle:** After fire and earthquake, God speaks not in the storm but in a whisper — rest after exhaustion, presence after fear
**Story beat summary:** Elijah, exhausted and afraid after confronting the prophets of Baal, flees into the wilderness. An angel feeds him. He walks forty days to Horeb. God tells him to stand on the mountain. A great wind, an earthquake, a fire pass — but God is not in them. Then comes a still, small voice. Elijah covers his face. God speaks gently and sends him forward with purpose.
