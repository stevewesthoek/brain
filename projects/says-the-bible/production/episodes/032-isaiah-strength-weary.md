# [032] Strength to the Weary — Isaiah 40 for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 032-isaiah-strength-weary-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Strength & Wings
**Bible passage:** Isaiah 40
**Angle:** Comfort, comfort my people — the God who measures the waters in the hollow of His hand gives power to the faint and strength to the weary
**Story beat summary:** Isaiah 40 opens with comfort. Every valley lifted, every mountain made low. The grass withers, the flower fades, but God's word stands forever. He tends His flock like a shepherd, carries the lambs. He does not faint or grow weary. Those who wait on the Lord shall renew their strength — they shall mount up with wings like eagles.
