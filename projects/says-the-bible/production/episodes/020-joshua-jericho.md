# [020] The Walls of Jericho Story for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 020-joshua-jericho-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Walls of Jericho
**Bible passage:** Joshua 6
**Angle:** A city taken not by siege but by silence and obedience — seven days of walking and waiting
**Story beat summary:** God tells Joshua to march around Jericho silently with the ark for six days. On the seventh day they march seven times. The priests blow the trumpets, the people shout, and the walls fall. The city is taken not by force of arms but by faith and obedience to God's unusual command.
