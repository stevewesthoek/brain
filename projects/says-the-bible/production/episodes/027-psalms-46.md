# [027] Psalm 46 — Be Still and Know for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 027-psalms-46-30m
**Thumbnail title:** Be Still & Know
**Bible passage:** Psalm 46
**Angle:** A psalm of refuge when everything shakes — God is our fortress, and the command to be still carries the weight of sovereign peace
**Story beat summary:** The psalm opens with God as refuge and strength. Even if the earth gives way and mountains fall into the sea, there is a river whose streams make glad the city of God. Nations rage, kingdoms totter, but God speaks and the earth melts. Then the command: Be still, and know that I am God. The Lord of hosts is with us.
