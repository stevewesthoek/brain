# [033] Daniel in the Lions' Den for Deep Sleep | Bible Bedtime Story | 30 Minutes

**Slug:** 033-daniel-lions-den-30m
**Thumbnail title:** The Lions' Den
**Bible passage:** Daniel 6
**Angle:** A man of prayer who would not stop praying, thrown to the lions and kept safe through the night — trust through the darkest hours
**Story beat summary:** Daniel serves faithfully under King Darius. His enemies trap him with a decree: pray to no one but the king for thirty days. Daniel kneels and prays as always. He is thrown into the den of lions. A stone seals the mouth. The king fasts through the night. At dawn he calls out — Daniel answers. God sent His angel and shut the lions' mouths.
