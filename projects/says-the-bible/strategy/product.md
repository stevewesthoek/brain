0. Core Principle

We are not selling audio files.
We are selling a bedtime routine system.

Each video = one SKU.
Library = compounding asset.
Bundle = primary revenue driver.

The entry product ($9) is one complete bedtime routine session.
The bundle ($29–$49) is everything needed for a consistent nightly routine.

Price increases reflect library depth, not feature gating.

Distribution model:

- YouTube is free discovery.
- The website converts visitors into routine system owners.

⸻

1. Product Layers

Single Routine Session (entry)

Position as: "A complete bedtime routine session"

Contains:
• Calm narration (30 minutes)
• Brown noise
• Structured pacing
• Repeatable format
• WAV master (24-bit / 48kHz) + MP3 (320 kbps) for offline use

Price (tiered by library depth):

• Launch phase (0–40 audios): $9
• Growth phase (40–80 audios): $12
• Established library (80–150+): $19

Goal: fast trust, low friction, long-term perceived value.

Public messaging rule:
Only the current price is shown. Do not mention the ladder or future increases.

⸻

Routine Library Bundle (PRIMARY REVENUE DRIVER)

Position as: "Everything needed for a consistent, peaceful bedtime routine"

Contains:
• All current stories
• All future releases at no extra cost
• Routine guidance

Price early: $29–$49
Price later: $49–$79

Bundle price scales slower than single SKU price.

Goal: Install the habit. The bundle makes switching frictionless.

⸻

Future Packs

Examples:
• Language packs
• Seasonal packs
• Age packs
• Extended night loops

Loop/ambient variants are separate SKUs, not part of the base single audio.

No subscriptions initially.

3. Upsell Logic

Post-purchase only.

Message:
Build a consistent routine.

Upgrade price = bundle minus single.

No countdowns.
No pressure.

Trust first.

⸻

4. SKU Naming Convention

Display format (public):

BOOK → STORY

Examples:
GENESIS → NOAH
PSALMS → REST
MARK → STORM

Internal SKU slug:

book-story-30m

Example:
genesis-noah-30m

Keeps library searchable and Stripe metadata stable.

⸻

5. Pricing Philosophy (Updated)

Low entry
Predictable increases
Library-driven value expansion

Key rule:

Price increases are explained as library growth, never scarcity.

Communication style:

Subtle transparency:

“Early library pricing.”

Do not use urgency language.
Do not mention deadlines.
Do not promise final price.

Simplicity signals seriousness.

⸻

6. Product Page Design Rules (New)

Each audio page must communicate:

Above the fold

• BOOK → STORY title
• One sentence emotional framing
• Calm hero imagery
• Price
• Ownership language (“yours permanently”)

Mid section

Explain:

• What the audio does
• When to use it
• Duration
• Formats included (WAV 24-bit/48kHz + MP3 320 kbps)
• Routine suggestion

Credibility section

• Production quality (WAV master)
• Method explanation (calm, non-scientific hype)

Conversion section

• Buy single
• Upgrade to library (soft)

No comparison tables.
No feature stacking.
