## What to Do
Okay, I'll guide you through setting up a 14-day free trial in GoHighLevel without collecting credit card information upfront, and then automatically prompting for payment details after the trial period. This is a bit more complex than a simple "credit card required" trial, but it's the best approach for maximizing signups, as we've discussed.\nGoHighLevel doesn't have a built-in, single-click "free trial without credit card" feature in the strictest sense. We'll need to combine several GHL features to achieve this:\nProducts: We'll create a "free" product for the trial.\nTriggers: We'll use triggers to automate the process.\nWorkflows (or Campaigns): To manage the communication and transition to paid.\nForms: To collect initial signup information.\nPipelines/Opportunities: To track the trial users.\nHere's a step-by-step guide:\nStep 1: Create a "Free Trial" Product\nGo to Payments > Products.\nClick "+ Add Product".\nProduct Name:  "ProChat MCTB - 14-Day Free Trial" (Be very clear!)\nProduct Description: "Experience the power of ProChat's Missed-Call Text-Back feature with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required."\nProduct Type: Service\nPricing:\nPricing Name: Free Trial\nPrice Display Override: Free\nAmount: Set this to $0.00.\nRecurring:  Set this to One time\nBilling Period: (Leave this blank, as it's a one-time $0 product)\nSave the product.\nStep 2: Create a Signup Form (if you don't have one already)\nGo to Sites > Forms > Builder.\nCreate a new form (or modify an existing one).\nInclude Fields:\nFull Name\nEmail Address\nPhone Number\nAdd a desclaimer that by submitting the form, users will receive text messages.\nForm Submission Settings:\n*   On Submit: Choose Open URL. Put here the url of a thank-you page. It is adviced to have a dedicated thank-you page.\nIntegrate: Copy the form link, or embed code.\nSave the form.\nStep 3: Create a Pipeline (Optional but Recommended)\nGo to Opportunities > Pipelines.\nCreate a new pipeline (or use an existing one).\nPipeline Name: "ProChat Trials" (or similar)\nStages:\nNew Trial Signup\nActive Trial (Days 1-13)\nTrial Expiring Soon (Day 14)\nTrial Expired\nConverted to Paid\nCancelled\nStep 4: Create a Workflow\nThis is the core of the automation. We'll use a workflow to manage the entire trial process.\nGo to Automation > Workflows.\nCreate a new workflow (Start from scratch).\nWorkflow Trigger:\nForm Submitted\nFilters:  Form is [Your Signup Form]\nActions:\nAction 1: Add Opportunity (Optional - if using Pipelines)\nPipeline:  [Your "ProChat Trials" Pipeline]\nStage: New Trial Signup\nAction 2: Add Tag\nTag: prochat-trial (or similar - this helps identify trial users)\nAction 3: Send Email (Welcome Email)\nFrom Name: [Your Name/Company Name]\nFrom Email: [Your Email Address]\nSubject: "Welcome to Your ProChat 14-Day Free Trial!"\nEmail Body:  Welcome the user, explain the key features, provide links to tutorials/help docs, and set expectations for the trial.\nAction 4: Wait\nWait for:  Time Delay\nDelay: 13 Days This puts them near the end of the trial.\nAction 5: Send Email (Trial Expiring Soon)\nFrom Name: [Your Name/Company Name]\nFrom Email: [Your Email Address]\nSubject: "Your ProChat Trial Ends Tomorrow!"\nEmail Body: Remind them the trial is ending, reiterate the benefits, and include a clear call to action to upgrade to a paid plan.  Include a direct link to your pricing page/checkout page (see Step 5).\nAction 6: Wait\nWait for:  Time Delay\nDelay: 1 Days This puts them at the end of the trial.\nAction 7: Send Email (Trial Expired)\nFrom Name: [Your Name/Company Name]\nFrom Email: [Your Email Address]\nSubject: "Your ProChat Trial Has Expired"\nEmail Body: Inform them their trial has ended.  Include a final call to action to upgrade to a paid plan, highlighting the benefits they'll lose if they don't upgrade. Include a link to your pricing/checkout page.\nAction 8: Remove Tag\nTag: prochat-trial (Remove the trial tag after expiration)\nAction 9: Remove from all Workflows.\nStep 5: Create Your Pricing/Checkout Page (Where Users Upgrade)\nThis is where users will go after the trial to enter their payment information and choose a paid plan. This page should clearly list your pricing options (the $97/month MCTB plan, and any other plans you offer).  You can build this page within GoHighLevel's Sites section (Funnels or Websites).  This page is crucial.\nUse the product you created in Step 1 on this checkout page. Since that product is $0.00, it won't be used for the actual purchase. It's just a trigger for the free trial.  You'll need to create separate products for your paid plans.\nMake sure the checkout page is secure (HTTPS).\nMake it responsive.\nStep 6: Handling the Upgrade (After Trial - IMPORTANT):\nWhen a user clicks the link to your pricing/checkout page (from the "Trial Expiring Soon" or "Trial Expired" emails), they'll choose a paid plan and enter their payment information. This is where the second product (the paid one) comes in.\nCreate a new product: with the $97,- price, and the settings on recurring.\nMake sure your funnel uses the newly created product.\nAutomation:\nCreate a second Workflow.\nWorkflow Trigger:\nOrder Form Submission\nFilters:  Submission Type is Sale\nFilters: Product is [Your "$97,- product"]\nFilters: Pipeline Stage is [Your "ProChat Trials" Pipeline]\nFilters: Pipeline is Trial Expired\nActions:\nAction 1: Update Opportunity Stage (Optional)\nMove the opportunity to the "Converted to Paid" stage.\nAction 2: Add Tag\nTag: prochat-paid\nAction 3: Remove Tag\nTag: prochat-trial (Remove the trial tag)\nAction 4: Send email (Thank you mail).\nAction 5: Create a task for yourself to check if the right product is bought.\nAction 6: Remove from all Workflows.\nUser experience:\nCreate a seperate thank-you page where people get redirected after they converted from trial user to paid member.\nImportant Considerations:\nTesting: Thoroughly test this entire workflow, from signup to trial expiration to upgrade, to ensure everything works as expected.\nEmail Deliverability: Ensure your emails are not landing in spam folders. Use a reputable email sending service (GoHighLevel provides this).\nLegal Compliance: Make sure your terms of service and privacy policy are up-to-date and compliant with relevant regulations.\nSupport: Be prepared to provide excellent support to trial users.\nThis detailed, step-by-step guide provides a robust framework for implementing a 14-day free trial without requiring credit card information upfront within GoHighLevel, followed by a clear path to upgrade to a paid plan. The key is using a combination of products, triggers, workflows, and a well-designed checkout page. Remember to test thoroughly! This is the best way to maximize free trial signups while still ensuring a smooth transition to paid subscriptions.
