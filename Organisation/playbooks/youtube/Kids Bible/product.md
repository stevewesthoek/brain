📄 PRODUCTS.md — Product Architecture (v0.1)

Status: Active
Purpose: Commercial structure and monetization logic.

This prevents pricing chaos and funnel sprawl.

⸻

0. Core Principle

Each video = one SKU.
Library = compounding asset.

We sell ownership, not access.

⸻

1. Product Layers

Single Audio (entry)

Contains:
• MP3 narration
• Extended loop
• Noise variants
• Printable card

Price: €9–€12

Goal: fast trust, low friction.

⸻

Library Bundle (core)

Contains:
• All audios
• Future additions
• Routine guidance

Price early: €29–€39
Price later: €49–€79

Goal: habit formation.

⸻

Future Packs

Examples:
• Language packs
• Seasonal packs
• Age packs
• Extended night loops

No subscriptions initially.

⸻

2. Funnel Hierarchy

Video
→ Specific audio page
→ Post-purchase upgrade
→ Library
→ Method page

Specific → system.

⸻

3. Upsell Logic

Post-purchase only.

Message:
Build a consistent routine.

Upgrade price = bundle minus single.

No countdowns.
No pressure.

Trust first.

⸻

4. SKU Naming Convention

Format:

Story-Theme-Length

Examples:
Noah-Bedtime-30m
Psalms-Sleep-45m
Promises-Loop-2h

Keeps library searchable.

⸻

5. Pricing Philosophy

Low entry
Predictable bundle
Gradual increases

Never complex tiers.

Simplicity signals seriousness.

⸻

6. Affiliate Layer

Devices recommended as routine tools.

Positioning:
Implementation guidance, not promotion.

Revenue secondary to trust.

⸻

7. Main Funnel Page Role

Explains:
• Method
• Devices
• Library
• Science framing
• Routine guidance

Product pages convert.
Funnel page builds belief.

⸻

8. Success Metrics

Important signals:

• Repeat buyers
• Bundle upgrades
• Library growth
• Device interest
• Routine adoption

Revenue follows routine adoption.

⸻

9. Failure Modes

Too many funnels
Overpricing early
Subscription too soon
SKU chaos
Scientific overclaiming
Aggressive upsells

Stability > optimization.

⸻

10. Long-Term Moat

Content library
Routine method
Hardware integration
Multilingual duplication

Products become infrastructure.

⸻

Final Summary

The product system converts individual bedtime audio into a growing library that families rely on nightly.

Ownership creates habit.
Habit creates revenue.
