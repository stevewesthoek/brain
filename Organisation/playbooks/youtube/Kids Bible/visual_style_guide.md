📘 SAYSTHE.BIBLE — VISUAL STYLE GUIDE v1.0

Purpose:
Create a coherent sensory environment that supports bedtime routines, signals seriousness, and scales across website, YouTube, products, and future apps.

This guide defines constraints.
Constraints create recognisability.

⸻

1. ICP — Emotional & Functional Needs

Primary ICP:
Christian parents of children ages 3–10 who want calmer bedtimes.

They are not seeking entertainment.
They are seeking relief, structure, and trust.

Key emotional needs:

• Reduce bedtime friction
• Provide safe content
• Avoid overstimulation
• Feel like a thoughtful parent
• Establish routine without screens
• Use something repeatable

Key sensory needs:

• Low stimulation
• Warm familiarity
• Soft lighting
• Predictable visuals
• Non-commercial feeling

The design must feel like entering a quiet room.

⸻

2. Brand Answer

Brand name: Saysthe.bible
Tagline: Calm Scripture for Bedtime Routines

Brand promise expressed visually:

Scripture becomes environment.

Not a message.
A place.

Visual tone keywords:

Calm
Timeless
Gentle authority
Warm
Quiet confidence
Editorial
Sacred but approachable

Avoid:

Playful chaos
Cartoon energy
Tech startup aesthetics
Minimalist coldness
Luxury drama

⸻

3. Core Aesthetic Philosophy

The brand uses Editorial Watercolor Stillness.

Definition:

Illustrations that look like pages from a calm children’s book, photographed in evening light.

Principles:

Stillness over action
Symbol over narrative
Texture over detail
Light over contrast
Negative space over density

Every asset should feel breathable.

⸻

4. Color System

Primary palette:

Indigo (anchor)
Deep navy (night environment)
Warm parchment (safety)
Muted gold (meaning)

Secondary palette:

Dusty blue
Soft sage
Warm gray

Color behavior:

• Gradients preferred over flat blocks
• Night bias (cool base + warm light)
• Never pure black
• Never pure white
• Saturation kept low

The palette should feel like dusk.

⸻

5. Typography

Tone: Book, not app.

Primary serif:
Used for Scripture, headings, brand statements
Elegant but readable

Secondary sans:
Used for interface, buttons, metadata

Rules:

• Generous spacing
• Large line height
• No heavy weights
• Avoid all caps except micro labels

Typography should slow reading slightly.

⸻

6. Imagery System (Most Important Layer)

Illustration style:

Soft watercolor editorial illustration.

Shared traits across all images:

• Gentle brush texture
• Subtle grain
• Minimal detail
• Soft directional lighting
• Night palette bias
• Symbolic focus
• Calm composition
• No expressive faces
• No dynamic motion

Subjects change.
Style never changes.

Core subject categories:

Biblical symbols
Night environments
Simple rooms
Sky / water / light
Quiet objects

Imagery must feel reusable and loopable.

⸻

7. Composition Template

Every image follows similar structure:

Primary subject center or low center
Large negative space
Light source subtle
Soft vignette
Depth through gradients not detail

The frame should feel stable when watched for 30 minutes.

⸻

8. Motion Language (Video Layer)

Motion goal: remove orientation reflex.

Allowed motion:

Slow zoom
Soft light breathing
Gentle particle drift
Very slow parallax

Not allowed:

Cuts
Scene switching
Character animation
Fast movement
Camera shake

Motion must be ignorable.

⸻

9. Website Translation

The website is not a store.
It is a quiet environment.

Homepage:

Large illustration hero
Minimal copy
Soft gradients
Calm spacing

Library page:

Grid of illustration tiles
Consistent framing
Bundle highlighted through warmth not contrast

Product pages:

Hero illustration
Preview audio
Very short copy
Routine framing

Device/setup page:

Slightly more instructional but same palette and texture

The website should feel like browsing a book at night.

⸻

10. YouTube Channel Identity

Channel banner:

Single illustration environment
Soft light
Minimal text

Channel grid:

All thumbnails share:

Same palette
Same texture
Same framing
Different subject

Scrolling the channel should feel like one continuous world.

⸻

11. Video Visual Template

Each video:

One illustration
Slow motion preset
Optional title intro fade
Loopable ending

Visual novelty is avoided intentionally.

Video is an animated illustration tile.

⸻

12. Thumbnail Formula

Subject symbol
Soft glow
Minimal title
Large negative space

No arrows
No faces
No bold contrast
No bright colors

Recognition over clickbait.

⸻

13. Product Visual System

Product tiles reuse video illustration.

Printable cards reuse illustration.

PDF uses illustration fragments.

Every asset shares visual DNA.

One illustration → five surfaces.

⸻

14. Prompt Architecture for Image Generation

Permanent style block:

watercolor editorial bedtime illustration, indigo night palette, soft warm light, minimal detail, calm children’s book aesthetic, subtle grain, timeless, gentle negative space, consistent brand illustration

Subject block changes per story.

This ensures infinite scalability without style drift.

⸻

15. Sensory Guardrails

Design must never:

Increase stimulation
Signal urgency
Feel commercial
Compete with audio
Introduce visual novelty mid-routine

If an element draws attention, it is too strong.

⸻

16. Evolution Rules

Style evolves slowly.

Allowed evolution:

Refined texture
Lighting nuance
Composition experimentation within template

Not allowed:

Style shifts
Palette shifts
Cartoon pivot
3D pivot
Photorealism pivot

Continuity is the moat.

⸻

Final Principle

Saysthe.bible is not designed to be watched.

It is designed to be trusted.

Visual consistency creates familiarity.
Familiarity creates safety.
Safety enables routine.
Routine is the product.
