0. Core Principle

Sleep improves with predictable routines.

This method delivers a repeatable bedtime environment using:

• Familiar stories
• Calm narration
• Soft ambience
• Screen-free audio
• Consistent sequencing

The goal is not novelty.
The goal is recognition.

When the brain recognizes a pattern, arousal decreases.

⸻

1. Scientific Foundation (Plain Language)

Evidence highlights several mechanisms:

• Bedtime routines act as sleep cues
• Familiar narratives reduce cognitive load
• Calm voice reduces stimulation
• Audio allows screen-free listening
• Repetition strengthens conditioning

This method applies those mechanisms through Scripture audio.

We communicate support, not guarantees.

⸻

2. The Bedtime Audio Routine

Recommended flow:
	1.	Lights dim
	2.	Audio starts
	3.	Child listens passively
	4.	Story ends or loops
	5.	Same experience next night

Consistency is more important than duration.

⸻

3. Age Range

Primary: 3–10
Secondary: family listening
Tertiary: adults preferring gentle narration

Younger children respond strongly to repetition.
Older children respond to story familiarity.

5. Implementation Guidance

Key tips:

• Volume low enough for passive listening
• Same voice nightly
• Avoid switching content frequently
• Use looping when needed
• Keep lighting consistent

Routine beats variety.

⸻

6. Audio Design Principles

Every track includes:

• Calm narration
• Soft ambience
• Optional noise layer
• Loopable ending

No sound effects.
No dramatic spikes.

Predictability creates safety.
