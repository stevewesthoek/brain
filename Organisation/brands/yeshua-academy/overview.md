# Yeshua Academy Overview

## Positioning
Yeshua Academy is a Christ-centered church and teaching ministry in the Netherlands, registered as both a church and an ANBI non-profit.

- Base: Netherlands.
- Legal status:
  - officially registered Christian church
  - officially registered ANBI non-profit (`Algemeen Nut Beogende Instelling`)

Core theological anchor: Jesus is Lord, fully God and fully man, and Scripture is the final authority for doctrine and practice.

## Mission and Role
- Mission:
  - preach the gospel of Jesus Christ
  - make disciples grounded in Scripture
  - equip believers for service in church, family, and society
- Role in ecosystem:
  - spiritual root system under ArkWare Solutions and ProChat
  - non-profit channel to serve churches, ministries, and non-profits
  - live reference environment for ministry-focused digital workflows

## Who Yeshua Academy Serves
- Believers and seekers who want clear Bible-based teaching.
- Church members and volunteers who need discipleship and practical equipping.
- Ministries and non-profits that can benefit from digital tools and operational support.

## Brand Personality
- Biblical: Scripture first, not trend-led.
- Clear and uncompromising: no dilution of core doctrine for popularity.
- Pastoral: truth applied with care to real lives.
- Practically focused: teaching tied to daily stewardship and service.

## Brand Promise
Yeshua Academy teaches and lives the gospel of Jesus Christ through clear Bible-based doctrine, discipleship, and service, for the growth of believers and the blessing of the wider community.
