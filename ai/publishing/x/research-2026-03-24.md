## Psychology of Engagement

- Replies weighted 75x more than likes by the algorithm — genuine conversation > passive consumption
- First 15-60 minutes critical — algorithm measures engagement velocity relative to impressions
- High-arousal emotions (awe, excitement, anger, surprise) spread faster than neutral/sad content
- Social currency: people share what makes THEM look smart, generous, or in-the-know
- Cognitive dissonance: surprising stats or counterintuitive facts compel sharing

## Reply Craft

- For < 1k followers, replying > original posts — "High-Volume Replying" is the most effective growth engine
- Reply to top 50 influencers in your niche
- Value-add patterns:
  - The Missing Step: add a tip the author missed
  - The Data/Story Backup: validate with your own numbers or anecdote
  - The Thread Summary: TL;DR for long threads
  - The Counterpoint: respectfully argue the opposite
- High-quality replies drive profile visits — ensure pinned tweet/bio converts visitors to followers

## Human vs AI — Linguistic Fingerprint

### AI Red Flags
- Connectors: "furthermore", "regarding", "moreover", "meanwhile"
- Instructional: "it's crucial", "it's fundamental", "to ensure", "is essential"
- Abstract fillers: "importance", "in relation", "reinforce"
- Uniform sentence length and cadence
- Overly positive, motivational, neutral tone
- Formal capitalization
- Standardized structure

### Human Markers
- Time-specific references: "Tuesday", "last November", "3am"
- Informal transitions: "so", "because", "then"
- Negative emotional intensity: anger, doubt, frustration
- "Netspeak" and casual fragments: "lol same", "cool. great.", "what if"
- Inconsistent capitalization
- Expressive punctuation: "???", "..."
- Greater variability in sentence length
- Cognitive doubt and tentative language

## Humanisation Techniques

### Punctuation/Spacing/Caps
- Omit terminal periods in short tweets
- Use repetitive/expressive punctuation (???, ...)
- Line breaks for rhythm, not just readability
- Lowercase for emphasis; inconsistent sentence case
- Avoid formal, standardized capitalization

### Authentic Errors
- Typos that feel spontaneous (not forced)
- Relatable pain markers ("my best tweet was a typo")
- Do NOT introduce hallucination-style errors
- Do NOT be repetitive in vocabulary

### Rhythm
- AI: uniform, standardized sentence lengths
- Human: mix extremely short fragments with longer complex sentences
- Create conversational flow with variability

### Natural Randomness
- Include negative emotions, not just positivity
- Show doubt, uncertainty, messy process
- "Build in public" energy: messy drafts, cold coffee, things that broke
- Avoid being assertive and certain on everything

## Community Culture — Indie Hackers / SaaS / Tech Founders

- Transparency > polish
- Share revenue numbers, specific failures, messy workspace
- Abstract one level up: don't just tweet about your tool, tweet about the problem space
- Relatable pain: ARR anxiety, algorithm chaos, launch panic
- Community rituals: polls, tagging beta testers, feature votes
- "Build in Public" as trust-building, not performance

## Variation — Preventing Formulaic Content

### 5 Hook Formulas
1. Bold Numerical Claim: "I made $300k in 6 months doing this"
2. Vulnerability Confession: "I was too embarrassed to admit this but..."
3. Pattern Interrupt: "Everyone says X. They're wrong"
4. Identity Targeting: "If you have under 1k followers, read this"
5. Specific Pain Point: "If your tweets get views but no followers, here's why"

### 3-Bucket Strategy
- Authority (expert insights)
- Personality (relatable struggles)
- Shareable (broadly applicable quotes)

### Repurposing
- Every 4-6 weeks, repost top-performing content with different hook/visual

## Do's and Don'ts

| Category | DO | DON'T |
|---|---|---|
| Psychology | Use "You" and "Your" | "RT if you agree" engagement bait |
| Replies | Add missing step or unique data | "Great point" / "totally agree" |
| Human vs AI | Use time/date markers ("Last Thursday") | "Furthermore", "consequently" |
| Culture | Share revenue and messy photos | Generic inspirational fluff |
| Structure | Line breaks, clear org | More than 1-2 hashtags |
| Links | Links in 2nd tweet or after traction | Spam links in original tweets |
