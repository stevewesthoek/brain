# Positioning

## ICP
- Small and mid-size organizations with operational bottlenecks that require custom systems.
- Non-profits and mission-driven teams needing reliable donation/member/process tooling.
- Agencies or B2B operators needing niche digital products.

## Differentiators
- Strong strategy-to-build continuity (not just implementation tickets).
- Bias for maintainability and long-term operating value.
- Product-lab relationship with ProChat for scalable handoff opportunities.

## Competitive alternatives
- One-off freelance implementation without strategic continuity.
- Generalist agencies with weak product operations depth.
- Overbuilt enterprise platforms with poor fit for smaller teams.
