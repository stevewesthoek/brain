# Organisation Ecosystem

High-level map of how ArkWare Solutions, ProChat, and Yeshua Academy fit together.

## Organizations
- `ArkWare Solutions`: Dutch-based digital studio for strategy, consulting, and bespoke digital solutions.
- `ProChat`: Portuguese-based product brand delivering packaged app products and services (`Apps`, `Kits`, `Studio`, plus platform endpoints like `Cloud` and `Status`).
- `Yeshua Academy`: Netherlands-based, officially registered Christian church and ANBI non-profit ministry.

## Relationship Model
- `Yeshua Academy` is the spiritual and non-profit foundation.
- `ArkWare Solutions` is the strategy and custom-build layer.
- `ProChat` is the packaged, repeatable product layer.

## Operating Flow
- Faith and mission context are anchored in `Yeshua Academy`.
- Strategy, architecture, and bespoke implementation happen in `ArkWare Solutions`.
- Productization and scalable distribution happen in `ProChat`.

In short: `Faith -> Strategy and Craft -> Scalable Products`.
