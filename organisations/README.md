# Organisations

Canonical organisation and brand context.

## Structure

- `conventions.md` — repo and information-architecture rules
- `ecosystem.md` — high-level business and entity map
- `arkware/` — Arkware positioning and offer material
- `prochat/` — ProChat company, growth, messaging, and legal material
- `yeshua-academy/` — Yeshua Academy messaging and offer material

## Rule

Organisation-level truth belongs here, not in `runtime/` or scattered notes.

Use lowercase kebab-case for organisation folders and keep each organisation internally grouped by function.
