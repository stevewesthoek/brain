# Positioning

## ICP
- Believers and seekers pursuing Scripture-grounded teaching.
- Church members and volunteers needing discipleship and practical equipping.
- Ministries/non-profits needing doctrinally grounded support structures.

## Differentiators
- Explicit biblical authority and doctrinal clarity.
- Registered church + ANBI non-profit legitimacy in the Netherlands.
- Practical discipleship orientation tied to everyday stewardship.

## Competitive alternatives
- Trend-led teaching ecosystems with weak doctrinal foundations.
- Generic motivational content disconnected from biblical discipleship.
- Ministry structures without operational clarity.
