# ProChat Customer Profiles

---

# Primary Customer

Non-technical founders who want to build SaaS.

They often have:

• niche expertise  
• market knowledge  
• business ideas

But lack:

• software architecture knowledge  
• infrastructure clarity  
• production confidence

---

# Common Pain Points

• not knowing where to start  
• fear of building the wrong thing  
• fragile technical systems  
• uncertainty about infrastructure decisions

---

# Goals

Customers want:

• clarity  
• structure  
• faster progress  
• reliable systems

---

# Builder Types

ProChat mainly attracts three builder types.

### Builder Starting From Zero

Someone with an idea but no technical background.

---

### Solo Builder With Limited Time

A founder building SaaS alongside other work.

---

### Niche Insider

Someone with deep industry knowledge who wants to build SaaS for their niche.
