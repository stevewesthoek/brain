# ProChat Offer Architecture

---

# Offer Philosophy

ProChat sells digital products designed to help founders build SaaS.

Most products are one-time purchases with lifetime updates.

---

# Core Products

## ProKit

Price: $147

A minimal SaaS boilerplate designed for maximum flexibility.

ProKit includes the technical foundation for building SaaS applications.

Best for founders who want full control over design and structure.

---

## SaaSKit

Price: $247

The flagship product.

SaaSKit includes:

• ProKit infrastructure  
• marketing system  
• SaaS launch foundation

This product allows founders to build and launch SaaS quickly.

---

# Upcoming Products

## UXKit

UI and design layer that can be applied to any repository.

Purpose:

Improve the visual quality of SaaS products.

---

## WaaSKit

A niche-focused system for founders who want to start with website-as-a-service.

WaaSKit provides:

• a niche funnel  
• example website templates  
• a validation strategy

This method allows founders to generate revenue and validate problems before building SaaS.

---

## ProChat OS

Future SaaS product.

A dashboard for monitoring and managing multiple SaaS products.

Possible features include:

• API monitoring  
• system overview  
• subscription monitoring  
• infrastructure status

---

# Ecosystem Strategy

All ProChat products are designed to work independently.

However they naturally form an ecosystem where founders may combine multiple tools.

Example path:

ProKit → SaaSKit → ProChat OS
