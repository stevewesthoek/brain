# Infinite Brain Intelligence Feedback Learning Loop

**Status:** MRU0-P3.8 report-only learning readiness
**Runtime boundary:** evidence analysis only; no model training, optimizer, autonomous learning, proposal, decision, write, or canonical update path

## Signals

The analyzer groups aggregate operational feedback into context effectiveness, continuity effectiveness, intelligence usefulness, and operational friction signals. It measures successes, failures, stale/conflicting state, accepted/rejected findings, prioritization usefulness, false positives/negatives where evidenced, uncertainty calibration, corrections, missing capabilities, and recurring manual work.

Each signal preserves evidence, timeframe, confidence, affected capability, uncertainty, Mind impact, recommended improvement area, explanation, and `action=report_only`.

## Authority boundary

Signals are evidence for future human review, not decisions, proposals, or change requests. Mind owns meaning, importance, strategy, and priorities. A possible Mind effect is surfaced with `mind_review_required=true`; Brain does not determine that it is important or update Mind.

## Safety and reuse

The loop consumes operational feedback projections and reuses existing observation, calibration, pattern, maintenance, prioritization, Context Broker, and continuity contracts. It creates no database or hidden scoring authority, ingests no transcripts, calls no providers, changes no clients, and has no write path.
