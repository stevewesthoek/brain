# Infinite Brain Operational Activation and Feedback Loop

**Status:** MRU0-P3.7 controlled report-only operational usage
**Runtime boundary:** capability inventory and aggregate feedback projection only; no scheduler, daemon, autonomous maintenance, decision, proposal, write, or provider path

## Activation states

Completed capabilities are exposed as `available`, `activated`, `pilot`, `disabled`, or `review_required`. The current bounded consumer inventory activates only validated read-only entry/context and continuity surfaces; maintenance and prioritization remain available for controlled use and human review.

## Feedback

Usage events are supplied as aggregate observations. The projection measures invocation frequency, usefulness, failed retrievals, stale context, continuity failures, false-positive findings, and possible Mind review. It stores nothing, ingests no transcripts, and does not infer human importance.

## Client boundary

Claude Code, Codex, and Workbench remain bounded consumers of the same Brain entry and continuity model. No new client is added. Brain remains the operational/policy/validation authority; Mind remains the meaning/importance/strategy authority.

## Go-live and rollback

Only validated reversible capabilities are activated. Autonomous writes, maintenance, decisions, Mind changes, session takeover, transcript ingestion, and provider mutation remain disabled. Stop the projection and restore prior client context paths to roll back; no persistent feedback state exists.
