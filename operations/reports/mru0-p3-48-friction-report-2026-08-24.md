# MRU0-P3.48 Operational Friction Report

## Observed friction

- The daily workflow still requires explicit operator execution; there is no scheduler.
- Seven of eleven real items remain pending, so review workload is visible but not reduced automatically.
- Human decision recording requires exact review ID, state, reason, reviewer, and matching source reference.
- The calibration layer reports false-positive measurement and preview usage as not instrumented, limiting quantitative value measurement.
- The learning checkpoint records review-summary evidence as a manual-step observation rather than a richer operator note.

## Interpretation

These are operational adoption costs, not critical blockers. They preserve safety and authority boundaries. The most important measurement gap is the lack of repeated real-session history; one checkpoint cannot establish durable workload or recall trends.

## Deferred improvements

Do not implement these during P3.48. Reconsider only after at least two weeks or ten completed review sessions, using real calibration and decision history:

- better review workload measurement;
- explicit operator friction capture;
- false-positive and preview-use instrumentation;
- clearer prioritization of pending items.

Automatic discovery, historical ingestion, autonomous action, and automatic promotion remain out of scope.
