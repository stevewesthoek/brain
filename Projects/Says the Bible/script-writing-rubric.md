📄 SCRIPT WRITING RUBRIC

Version: v1.3
Date: 2026-03-01
Purpose: Define how to write a Saysthe.bible script that is calm, loopable, and consistent across the library.

Canonical references: `bedtime_scripture_format.md`, `script-template.md`

⸻

## 0) Core Ratio (30-Minute English Track)

Target ratio by time:

- 85% narrative (faithful paraphrase + story flow)
- 15% reflective calming application

For a 30-minute track:

- Narrative: ~25–26 minutes
- Reflection: ~4–5 minutes

Reflection is woven after each story unit plus a final reflective loop.

No verbatim Scripture and no verse numbers or citations in the script.

⸻

## 1) Narrative Voice

- Slow, gentle, concrete.
- Short sentences, present tense.
- Reassuring, not performative.
- No dramatic spikes or suspense.

Approved patterns:

- Soft sensory anchors: `the night is quiet`, `the room is still`.
- Gentle repetition: `you are safe`, `you are carried`.
- Low-demand listening: `you can rest while you listen`.

Avoid:

- Exclamation points.
- Surprise turns or cliffhangers.
- High-energy verbs (rush, explode, crash).
- Teaching language or sermon tone.

⸻

## 2) Text Fidelity (Paraphrase Rules)

- The story must be faithful to the Biblical text.
- Paraphrase only; never quote.
- Do not add details not in the text.
- Keep phrasing calm and child-level.

⸻

## 3) The Says the Bible Reflection Framework™

Every reflection block follows this order:

1. Observation
2. Meaning
3. Personal Connection
4. Permission to Rest

Never change the order.

Example pattern:

- Observation: “God spoke, and light appeared.”
- Meaning: “Light came because He chose it.”
- Personal Connection: “Light still comes when it is dark.”
- Permission to Rest: “You do not need to make the light. You can rest.”

Use this loop 5–7 times per episode, slightly slower each time.

⸻

## 4) Final 3-Minute Sleep Formula (Identical Every Episode)

The last segment always follows this structure:

1. Recap in 7–10 short lines
2. “It was good” pattern
3. “You were made” anchor
4. Triple rest permission

Template:

“God made light.
God made sky.
God made land.
God made life.
And He saw that it was good.”

Pause.

“You were made.
You are part of what is good.”

Pause.

“You do not need to hold the world together.
You can rest.

You do not need to make tomorrow happen.
You can rest.

You are safe.
You can rest.”

⸻

## 5) Structure And Reflection Weave

Arrival (0:00–2:30):

- Soft, nervous-system downshift.
- Reflection may appear briefly (safety language), but no doctrine.

Story Units (2:30–20:00):

- Each unit contains narrative + short reflection loop.
- Reflection loop uses the 4-step framework.

Anchor Block (20:00–24:00):

- Emotional center of the story.
- Reflection is stronger but still calm and short.

Rest Block (24:00–27:00):

- Reflection-heavy, low energy.
- Emphasize rest as part of creation.

Sleep Loop (27:00–30:00):

- Use the identical final formula.
- No new content.

⸻

## 6) Bedtime Fit Checklist

A script is acceptable only if:

- Tone never spikes.
- A child can fall asleep mid-sentence without missing a payoff.
- The closing loops cleanly.
- Reflection loops follow the 4-step order.
- The ratio stays within target.
