# Script Generator Checklist — v1.4
Purpose: Turn any Bible story into a 30-minute sleep-optimized episode.

Use this every time. Do not skip steps.

---

# PHASE 1 — STORY EXTRACTION (10–15 min)

□ 1. Identify the core narrative units (max 6)
   - Natural story breaks
   - Each unit must stand alone

□ 2. Write a one-sentence summary of the story
   (This becomes the Anchor Block theme)

□ 3. Identify the primary theological truth
   (Example: "God creates with intention")
   Only one. Not five.

□ 4. Define the identity statement
   (Example: "You are made on purpose")

If you cannot summarize the story clearly,
you are not ready to write it.

---

# PHASE 2 — STRUCTURAL PLANNING (5–10 min)

□ 5. Map 6 story units (2–2.5 min each)

□ 6. Plan reflection theme per unit:
   Unit 1: Observation focus
   Unit 2: Meaning focus
   Unit 3: Stability focus
   Unit 4: Care focus
   Unit 5: Intention focus
   Unit 6: Identity focus

Reflection loop order NEVER changes:
1. Observation
2. Meaning
3. Personal connection
4. Permission to rest

□ 7. Confirm narrative stops at minute 20.

No new imagery after this line.

---

# PHASE 3 — WRITING (20–40 min)

## 0:00–2:30 Arrival

□ Slow breathing cue
□ Reassurance line
□ No complex theology
□ No imagery spikes

---

## 2:30–20:00 Narrative Core

Per unit:

□ 2–2.5 min narrative
□ 30–45 sec reflection loop
□ Reflection ends with “You can rest.”
□ Decrease descriptive complexity gradually
□ Reduce sentence length by Unit 6

---

## 20:00–25:00 Integration Block

□ No new story content
□ Restate core truth
□ Restate identity statement
□ Increase pause density
□ Shorter sentences
□ More “you are safe” language

---

## 25:00–30:00 Sleep Loop

Use identical structure every episode.

Wave 1:
□ Core summary phrases
□ Moderate pauses

Wave 2:
□ Same phrases
□ Longer pauses

Wave 3:
□ Same phrases
□ Longest pauses
□ No new wording

---

# PHASE 4 — PAUSE CODING (10–15 min)

□ Apply P1 to most sentence ends
□ Apply P2 to scene changes
□ Apply P3 to reflection lines
□ Apply P4 to “You can rest.”
□ Insert P5 only in final 5 minutes
□ Increase pause duration progressively

Never insert P5 before minute 24.

---

# PHASE 5 — VOICE CONTROL

□ Speaking rate slightly slow (-10% to -15%)
□ Slightly lower pitch (-2% to -4%)
□ No emotional spikes
□ No dramatic emphasis
□ Calm, even delivery

---

# PHASE 6 — QUALITY CONTROL

Before exporting:

□ No new content after minute 20
□ No new theology in final 10 minutes
□ Reflection density increases after minute 20
□ Final 5 minutes are repetitive and predictable
□ Identity statement appears at least 3 times
□ “You can rest.” appears in every reflection loop

If any of these fail:
Revise before rendering.

---

# PHASE 7 — RUNTIME TARGETS

Target total length: 28–32 minutes

If under 27 minutes:
Add reinforcement, not new content.

If over 33 minutes:
Trim narrative, not reflection.

---

# FINAL RULE

You are not writing a story.
You are designing neurological descent.

Narrative → Meaning → Identity → Rhythm → Silence
