Why this works

Bedtime research highlights several factors that improve sleep:

• Consistent routines signal the brain that sleep is approaching
• Familiar stories reduce cognitive load and anxiety
• Calm narration lowers stimulation before bed
• Audio allows listening without screens or visual distraction

This channel combines those elements into a simple, repeatable format.
