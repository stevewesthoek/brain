# Full-Time Creator Playbook

## Summary
This playbook outlines 10 actionable steps to become a full-time creator in under four months. It covers niche selection, profile optimization, audience engagement, content creation strategies, and monetization through lead magnets, low-ticket courses, and high-ticket services, emphasizing leveraging authority and building in public.

## Key Points
- Pick a specific niche for faster growth and learning.
- Optimize online profiles to clearly state value and target audience.
- Build in public by sharing wins, losses, and milestones.
- Engage actively and consistently with target audiences.
- Write daily for increased reach, growth, and practice, batching content.
- Borrow authority by writing about established figures or popular topics.
- Develop lead magnets and offer giveaways for growth and authority.
- Do free work for one great case study to build skills and leverage.
- Create a low-ticket course as an easy entry point for passive income and upsell.
- Offer a high-ticket service (consulting, copywriting) for significant income with few clients.
