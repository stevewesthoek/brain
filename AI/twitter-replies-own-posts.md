# Twitter Replies — Own Post Context

This file applies when someone replied to Steve's own tweet/post and Steve wants a response.

## Goal

Handle replies under Steve's own posts in a way that:

- strengthens his position
- keeps the thread interesting
- closes low-value discussions when needed
- avoids wasting time
- shows confidence

---

## Important Distinction

Replies under Steve's own posts are NOT the same as feed replies.

These replies should often do one of three things:

1. deepen the thread
2. close the loop
3. shut down weak discussion cleanly

---

## Closing Rule

Sometimes the correct move is a closing reply.

A closing reply:
- does not invite long back-and-forth
- ends the exchange cleanly
- keeps tone controlled
- avoids sounding defensive

Examples of intent:
- "that’s the point"
- "exactly why this matters"
- "fair, but that's not the real bottleneck"
- "yes, and most people still miss it"

---

## Reply Style Mix

When Steve asks for reply options, default to 6 replies with variation such as:
- one engaging
- one sharper
- one witty
- one factual
- one authority-building
- one closing

---

## Avoid

- defensive tone
- emotional overreaction
- trying to win every argument
- long explanations
- sounding like support staff
