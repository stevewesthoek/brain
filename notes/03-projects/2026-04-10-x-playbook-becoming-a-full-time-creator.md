# X Playbook: Becoming a Full-Time Creator

## Summary
This note presents a 10-step "X playbook" designed to help individuals become full-time creators within four months, covering strategies from niche selection and profile optimization to content creation, audience engagement, and monetization through services and courses.

## Key Points
- Focus on selecting a niche, optimizing your profile, and building in public for rapid brand growth.
- Prioritize consistent daily content creation and active audience engagement (e.g., 20+ comments 3 times daily).
- Leverage 'borrowed authority' and develop lead magnets to attract and grow your audience effectively.
- Implement a tiered monetization strategy, progressing from free work and giveaways to low-ticket courses and high-ticket services.
- The playbook is framed as a time-bound project to achieve full-time creator status within four months.
