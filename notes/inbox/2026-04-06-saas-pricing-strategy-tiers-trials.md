# SaaS Pricing Strategy: Tiers & Trials

This note captures insights from a discussion on B2B SaaS product pricing, focusing on strategies to optimize margins, user acquisition, and overall business health.

## Key Insights & Recommendations

*   **Freemium's Impact:** A significant takeaway was that freemium models, while seemingly beneficial for initial adoption, tend to severely erode margins at scale, making them unsustainable for long-term growth and profitability.

*   **Usage-Based Pricing:** The recommended approach is to adopt a usage-based pricing model. This structure ensures that as customers derive more value, their contribution to revenue increases, while also incorporating a clear minimum floor to protect baseline revenue.

*   **Suggested Tiered Structure:**
    *   **Small Teams:** Start with an entry-level tier at **$49/month**.
    *   **Growing Businesses:** Offer a more comprehensive tier starting at **$199/month**.

*   **Annual Discounts:** To encourage longer-term commitments and improve customer lifetime value, a **20% discount** should be offered for annual subscriptions.

*   **Trial Over Free Tier:** Instead of a permanent free tier, implement a **14-day free trial**. This allows potential customers to fully experience the product's value without devaluing the offering or negatively impacting future conversion rates and margins.
