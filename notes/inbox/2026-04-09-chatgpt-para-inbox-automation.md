# ChatGPT PARA Inbox Automation Project

This note details the requirements and goals for automating the classification of ChatGPT conversation content into the PARA framework. The objective is to enhance a personal knowledge management system by automatically sorting raw conversation output, thereby eliminating manual effort and improving efficiency.

## Project Goal
Develop an n8n workflow to capture raw text content from ChatGPT conversations and classify it into one of four PARA categories: Project, Area, Resource, or Inbox. This directly supports the owner's goal of automating everything and optimizing work efficiency.

## Key Process Steps
1.  **Content Capture:** Obtain raw text output from ChatGPT conversations.
2.  **PARA Classification:** Determine the most appropriate PARA category based on the conversation's core topic and intent.
3.  **Insight Extraction:** Identify and summarize the key insights or decisions from the conversation.
4.  **Tagging:** Assign relevant keywords and tags for easy retrieval and cross-referencing.
5.  **Structuring:** Format the extracted information into a structured markdown note body, suitable for quick review and integration into the PKM system.

## Expected Outcome
-   Reduced manual sorting and organization time for ChatGPT outputs.
-   Consistent and structured knowledge capture across all AI interactions.
-   Enhanced efficiency and searchability within the personal knowledge management system.
