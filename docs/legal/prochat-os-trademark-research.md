# ProChat OS Trademark Research

**Status:** preliminary research, not legal clearance  
**Date:** 2026-05-23  
**Owner:** Steve Westhoek  
**Mark researched:** ProChat OS, ProChat

## Summary

`ProChat OS` is usable as a working product name, but it should be treated as medium-risk until a trademark attorney performs a formal clearance search.

The risk is not mainly the exact phrase `ProChat OS`. The risk is the existing crowded use of `ProChat` for chat, messaging, AI assistant, SaaS, and software products.

## Preliminary findings

### Exact phrase: ProChat OS

No obvious exact-match conflict was found in broad web searches during this pass.

That does not mean the mark is legally clear. Trademark conflicts can arise from similar marks, similar goods/services, phonetic similarity, translations, prior unregistered use, and country-specific rights.

### Root phrase: ProChat

The root term `ProChat` is already used publicly by several software/chat/AI products and services.

Examples found during preliminary search:

- `Prochat` mobile/business communication apps on app stores.
- `ProChat` AI assistant/product listings.
- `Pro-Chat` SaaS project references in Europe.
- Indian trademark record for `PROCHAT` in Class 9 for mobile/computer application software.
- Existing ProChat business presence at `prochat.tools` and related brand assets.

## Risk assessment

| Risk area | Level | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Exact `ProChat OS` collision | Low to medium | No obvious exact match found in broad search. |
| Similar `ProChat` software/chat marks | Medium to high | Several existing uses in adjacent software/chat/AI categories. |
| Descriptiveness | Medium | `Pro` + `Chat` can sound descriptive for chat software. `OS` adds distinctiveness if positioned beyond chat. |
| Confusion with generic chat apps | Medium | Avoid presenting ProChat OS as a team chat or customer chat product. |
| Protectability of `ProChat` alone | Lower | Crowded field may weaken enforceability. |
| Protectability of full `ProChat OS` | Better | Stronger as a combined mark if used consistently. |

## Recommended positioning to reduce risk

Use the full mark consistently:

```text
ProChat OS
```

Do not position the product as a generic chat app. Position it as:

```text
The source-available AI builder operating layer for memory, agents, automations, runtimes, and managed workflows.
```

Avoid primary category labels such as:

- business chat app
- team chat app
- customer chat platform
- chatbot platform

Prefer category labels such as:

- AI builder operating system
- AI workflow operating layer
- structured AI operations platform
- local-first AI control plane
- managed AI builder infrastructure

## Recommended trademark classes to review with counsel

Potential classes for filing strategy:

- **Class 9:** downloadable software, CLI tools, developer tools.
- **Class 42:** SaaS, hosted software, cloud management, software development tools, managed platform services.
- **Class 38:** only if the product is positioned as communication/messaging infrastructure. Avoid unless needed because this increases overlap with chat apps.
- **Class 41:** only if training/courses become a major branded product line.

## Filing strategy

Recommended path:

1. Continue using `ProChat OS` internally and in planning docs.
2. Commission a proper knockout and clearance search before public launch.
3. Prioritize EU/Portugal, US, and UK depending on launch markets.
4. File for `ProChat OS` as a word mark if counsel confirms acceptable risk.
5. Keep logo/design filings secondary until the word mark path is understood.
6. Add a public `TRADEMARKS.md` policy before publishing the code.

## Trademark policy recommendation

A public repo should say:

```text
ProChat, ProChat OS, and related logos are trademarks of Steve Westhoek / ProChat.
The source code license does not grant trademark rights.
You may not use the ProChat or ProChat OS names to identify a fork, hosted service, commercial service, or derivative product without written permission.
```

## Naming fallback options

If formal clearance shows `ProChat OS` is too crowded, consider names that preserve the strategy but reduce chat-category confusion:

- ProChat Core
- ProChat Engine
- ProChat Brain
- StructureOS
- BuilderOS by ProChat
- ProChat Control Plane
- ProChat Operator

## Decision

Proceed with `ProChat OS` as the working brand, but do not treat it as legally cleared until counsel reviews official trademark databases and marketplace use.
